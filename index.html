Privacy Policy

BugOff — Smash the Flies

📅 Last updated: March 2026

📋 Introduction

Welcome to BugOff. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile game.

By playing BugOff, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app.

📦 Information We Collect

We collect minimal data to provide core game functionality:

  • Username — chosen by you at account creation, stored in our database
  • Password — stored in our database (we recommend using a unique password)
  • Game progress — high score, coins, power-ups, levels reached
  • Device data — basic device info used for crash reporting and performance
We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information beyond your chosen username.

⚙️ How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Save and restore your game progress across devices
  • Maintain your coin balance and owned items
  • Display your account profile and task progress
  • Improve game performance and fix bugs

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

📺 Advertising (Coming Soon)

📺 🎯 💰

BugOff currently uses simulated ads to reward players with coins. In a future update, we plan to integrate real advertisements (such as Google AdMob rewarded ads) into the game.

When ads are enabled, third-party advertising networks may collect:

  • Advertising ID (Android GAID)
  • Approximate location (country/region level)
  • Device type and OS version
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)
We will update this Privacy Policy before enabling real ads and notify users through an in-app message. You will always have the option to limit ad tracking through your device settings.

🪙 In-App Purchases

BugOff includes optional in-app purchases processed through Google Play. When you make a purchase:

  • Payment is handled entirely by Google Play — we never see your payment details
  • Purchased coins are stored in your account in our database
  • All purchases are final — coins are non-refundable virtual currency
In-app purchases are currently disabled. This feature will be enabled in a future update after proper testing and approval.

🗄️ Data Storage & Security

Your game data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider. Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

Game progress is also cached locally on your device using AsyncStorage for offline functionality.

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure. We recommend using a unique password for your BugOff account.

👶 Children's Privacy

BugOff is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

⚖️ Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Update your username through the app

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

🔗 Third-Party Services

BugOff uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • Google Play — app distribution and in-app purchases
  • RevenueCat — purchase management (future use)
  • Google AdMob — advertising (future use)

Each service has its own privacy policy which governs their data practices.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes — especially before enabling real ads or purchases — we will notify you through an in-app message.

Continued use of BugOff after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
