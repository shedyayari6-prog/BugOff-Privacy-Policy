Privacy Policy

📋 Introduction

Welcome to BugOff. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you play our mobile game, including all features such as the Coin Store, Loot Wheel, Discover Mode, Card Market, and account system.

By playing BugOff, you agree to the practices described below. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app.

📦 Information We Collect

We collect minimal data to provide core game functionality:

  • Username & Password — chosen by you at account creation, stored securely in our database
  • Game progress — high score, coins, XP, level, power-ups (bombs, freezes), character unlocks
  • Discover Mode collection — enemies you have discovered and kill counts
  • Loot Wheel data — spin count, pity counter, legendary cards obtained
  • Coin Store progress — how many ads you have watched toward each coin pack
  • Card Market activity — cards sold and coins received
  • Task progress — daily and permanent task completion and claim status
  • Device data — basic device info used for crash reporting and performance
We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information beyond your chosen username.

📺 Advertising — Active

BugOff uses Google AdMob rewarded ads. You can watch ads in the Coin Store to earn free coins, and on the Game Over screen to continue playing or double your coins. Ads are optional — you are never forced to watch one.

When you watch an ad, Google AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID (Android GAID / iOS IDFA)
  • Approximate location (country / region level)
  • Device type, OS version, and language
  • Ad interaction data (views, completions, clicks)
  • App usage context for ad relevance
You can limit ad tracking at any time in your device settings: Settings → Privacy → Advertising (iOS) or Settings → Google → Ads (Android). Limiting tracking does not remove ads but may make them less relevant.

Google AdMob's full privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

🎰 Loot Wheel & Virtual Gambling Mechanics

BugOff includes a Loot Wheel (Spin Wheel) where players spend in-game coins — a virtual currency with no real-world monetary value — for randomized rewards including coins, power-ups, and collectible legendary cards.

  • Spin costs: 500 / 2,000 / 4,000 coins depending on spin type
  • Rewards are randomized using weighted probability tables stored in our backend
  • A pity system guarantees an Epic reward at 50 spins
  • Legendary cards are one-time collectibles controlled by our backend — once obtained, they cannot be obtained again from the wheel
  • All coin balances, spin counts, and pity counters are stored in your account on our servers
All rewards from the Loot Wheel are virtual in-game items only. Coins and cards have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for real money.

🔍 Discover Mode

Discover Mode is a special game mode that costs 2,500 in-game coins to enter. Enemies in this mode are loaded from our backend database, which may include images stored in cloud storage.

  • When you kill a new enemy for the first time, it is permanently added to your collection in our database
  • Kill counts and discovery timestamps are recorded per player
  • Enemy data (names, images, rarity, spawn rates) is managed by us from the backend and may change over time
  • Retired enemies (marked inactive) are no longer spawned but remain in your collection permanently
Your discovery collection is tied to your account and persists even if an enemy is later retired from the game.

🃏 Card Market

The Card Market allows you to sell discovery cards and legendary cards for in-game coins. Sell prices are fixed by rarity (Common: 50, Rare: 1,000, Epic: 2,500, Legendary: 75,000 coins).

  • For discovery cards, you can sell 1 to all of your kill count copies
  • Legendary cards are one-time items and are permanently deleted from your collection when sold
  • All sell transactions update your account in our database in real time
  • Sold cards cannot be recovered

🪙 Coin Store — Watch Ads to Earn Coins

The Coin Store lets you earn free coins by watching rewarded ads. Each coin pack requires watching a set number of ads (10–25) before the coins can be claimed.

  • Your ad watch progress for each pack is saved in your account on our servers
  • Progress is restored when you log in on a different device
  • Coins are credited to your account only after you claim a completed pack
Ad viewing is always voluntary. You can play BugOff without ever watching an ad.

💳 In-App Purchases

BugOff includes optional in-app purchases for coins and other items, processed through Google Play. When you make a purchase:

  • Payment is handled entirely by Google Play — we never see your payment details
  • Purchased coins are credited to your in-game account
  • All in-app purchases are for virtual currency and items with no real-world value
In-app purchases are currently pending Google Play approval. This feature will be enabled in a future update.

⚙️ How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Save and restore your game progress, coins, XP, and level across devices
  • Track your Discover Mode collection and Loot Wheel history
  • Persist your Coin Store ad progress so you don't lose watch counts
  • Maintain your Card Market sell history and balance
  • Display task progress and claim status
  • Improve game performance and fix bugs

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

🗄️ Data Storage & Security

Your game data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider with encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.

Game progress is also cached locally on your device using AsyncStorage for offline functionality. Local data is not shared with third parties.

We take reasonable steps to protect your data. We recommend using a unique password for your BugOff account.

👶 Children's Privacy

BugOff is designed for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

The Loot Wheel and card collection mechanics use virtual coins only — no real money is involved in these features. However, parents should be aware that the game contains randomized reward mechanics.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
